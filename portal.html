How portal access works ›

There are no access tiers. Access follows what a client has done. Two steps: first they need a portal login (your Invite button — until then they can't enter at all); then, once inside, they see the pieces below.

Always availableevery client

The Map (public on the website), Tapestry, and their own Signals reading once they've completed one.

After a Lineage Session, or manualyou flip the switch

Their Weaving, Catalog, and Convergence — turned on per client in the Portal Access card on their page.

Not yet availableoff for everyone

The Fieldguide — off until it is published.

Each client's page shows exactly where they stand: whether they have a login, what they've completed, and which pieces are on.

Researching the ancestor…
Disambiguation · Documentation · Frequency mapping

The ancestors a client names at intake arrive here. Research holds the work underneath the Weaving: source findings (FamilySearch, parish registers, archives), private notes on relationships and identity, and an assessment of how much record exists. When the research shows who belongs in the Weaving, Weave writes the ancestor from the findings into that client's Weaving only, never the shared archive. Hold keeps the research and hides the ancestor from the client. Set maternal or paternal before weaving.
